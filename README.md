##  Global E-commerce Sales Analysis

# Project Overview
This project analyzes global e-commerce sales data to uncover key business insights across product categories, regions, and time trends.

# Objectives
- Analyze total revenue and sales distribution  
- Identify top-performing product categories  
- Evaluate regional sales performance  
- Detect sales trends over time  
- Discover high-revenue products  

# Tools & Technologies
- Python  
- Pandas  
- Matplotlib  
- Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab
# Dataset
- File: global_ecommerce_sales.csv  
- Contains order, product, region, quantity, and pricing details
  
# Data Cleaning
- No missing values  
- No duplicate records  
- Converted date column to datetime  
- Created new column: Total_Sales = Quantity × Unit_Price  

# Key Insights

 # 🔸 Overall Revenue
- Total Revenue: ₹530,470  

# 🔸 Category Performance
- Furniture contributes ~53% of total sales  
- Technology is second highest  
- Office Supplies has very low contribution  

# 🔸 Regional Performance
- Europe and North America are top regions  
- Asia Pacific performs moderately  
- South America and Middle East & Africa are weak  

# 🔸 Top Products
- Furniture and office setup products dominate  
- High demand for desks and chairs  

# 🔸 Sales Trend
- Monthly sales range between ₹8K–₹20K  
- Indicates seasonal and inconsistent demand  

# Visualizations
- Sales by Category  
- Sales by Region  
- Top Products  
- Monthly Sales Trend  

# Conclusion
- Business depends heavily on Furniture category  
- Some regions are underperforming  
- Sales show seasonal fluctuations
